Official Giveaway Rules & Eligibility

November 12, 2026 – December 25, 2026

1. Who can enter

Entrants must be 18 years of age or older. The event receiving the prize must take place within 40 miles of downtown Buffalo, New York.

2. Entry period

The giveaway opens on November 12, 2026 and closes at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on December 25, 2026. Entries submitted after the closing time will not be included in the drawing.

3. How to enter

No purchase is necessary to enter or win. To be eligible, complete all of the following steps during the entry period:

  • Like the Nickel City Clicks giveaway post on Instagram.
  • Follow @nickelcityclicks on Instagram.
  • Tag a friend in the giveaway post comments who needs a photo booth at their event.
  • Complete and submit the giveaway entry form on this page.

Entries must satisfy all of the requirements above to be eligible.

4. Prize

One winner will receive the Full Photo Booth Package for one event lasting up to six hours. The event must take place within 40 miles of downtown Buffalo, New York. No cash alternative or additional prize details are stated in this promotion.

5. Winner selection and notification

One eligible winner will be selected at random from the qualifying entries. The winner will be announced on January 1, 2027 and contacted using the email address provided in the giveaway form.

6. Email and privacy

Information submitted through the entry form will be used to administer the giveaway and contact the potential winner. You will only be added to the Nickel City Clicks marketing email list if you separately check the optional mailing-list consent box. Leaving that box unchecked will not affect your giveaway entry.
